## Discussion Guide

### Intro (5 min)
Introduce yourself, explain the purpose, get recording consent.

### Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me a bit about your role and how you use [product area].

### Core Questions (30-40 min)
1. Walk me through the last time you [relevant task].
2. What was the hardest part of that?
3. What did you do when [specific scenario]?

### Wrap-up (5 min)
- Is there anything else you wanted to share?
- Would you be open to follow-up questions?
