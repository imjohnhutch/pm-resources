## TL;DR

Three sentences. What does this product do, who is it for, and what's the most interesting thing about how it's built?

## Why I'm tearing this down

What I'm trying to learn. Examples:
- Studying their onboarding flow because I'm rebuilding ours
- Understanding how they handle [X problem]
- Benchmarking against a competitor

Being explicit about the question keeps the teardown focused.

## The product

### What it does
Plain-English description. No marketing copy.

### Who it's for
The actual user, not the marketing persona. What job are they hiring this for? (Jobs to Be Done framing helps.)

### How they make money
Pricing model, tiers, what's gated. If freemium, where's the upgrade pressure?

## The experience

### First 60 seconds
Walk through what a new user sees from landing page to first meaningful action. What did they ask me to do, in what order, and why?

### The "aha" moment
Where does a new user first feel the value? How quickly do they get there? What gets in the way?

### Core loop
The thing the user does repeatedly. Map the steps. Note friction, delight, or surprises.
