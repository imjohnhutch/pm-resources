# PM Resources

A personal collection of templates, frameworks, and links I keep coming back to. Less a curated list, more a living reference doc.

## Templates

| Template | What it is |
|---|---|
| PRD | Standard product requirements doc — problem, goals, scope, open Qs |
| One-pager | Lightweight brief for early-stage ideas |
| Research plan | Interview and usability study planning |
| Launch checklist | Pre-launch and post-launch steps |
| Retro | Sprint and launch retrospective format |

Templates are in `/templates/`.

## Frameworks I Use

**Discovery**
- Jobs to Be Done — for understanding what users are actually hiring your product for
- Continuous Discovery (Teresa Torres) — running ongoing customer interviews
- JTBD interview script — structured but conversational

**Prioritization**
- RICE scoring (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort)
- Opportunity Solution Tree — mapping opportunities to potential solutions
- MoSCoW for quick stakeholder alignment

**Writing**
- Working backwards from the press release (Amazon-style)
- Situation / Complication / Resolution for exec communication
- Pre-mortem before major launches

## Notes on usage

These templates are starting points, not constraints. I adapt them for each situation — a lightweight feature might only need the one-pager, while a major initiative gets the full PRD + research plan. The goal is to write just enough to align the team, not to produce documentation for its own sake.
